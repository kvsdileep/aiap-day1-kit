## Studies

| Study | Used in | What it found |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bastani et al., "Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning", PNAS 2025 ([Wharton summary](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/without-guardrails-generative-ai-can-harm-education), [journal listing](https://ideas.repec.org/a/nas/journl/v122y2025pe2422633122.html)) | 1.5 | About 1,000 high school maths students in Turkey. Plain ChatGPT: 48% better on practice, 17% worse on the exam without AI. Hint-only tutor: 127% better on practice, about the same as no AI on the exam. |
| [Anthropic, "How AI assistance impacts the formation of coding skills", 29 Jan 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-assistance-coding-skills) | 1.5 | 52 engineers learning the Trio library. AI group averaged 50% on the quiz, hand-coding group 67%. Those who asked conceptual questions scored 65% or more. Those who delegated the coding scored under 40%. Small sample. The usage-pattern finding is not causal. |
| Doshi and Hauser, Science Advances 2024 ([dataset page](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.qfttdz0pm)) | 1.1 | AI story ideas made stories more novel but more similar to each other. The scripts quote no figure. Verify the paper before quoting a number. |
