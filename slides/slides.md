# AIAP Day 1: From talking to AI to working with AI

Deck source for the Day 1 slides. The HTML is built from this file, so slide numbers match. Design family: `intl-workshop` (Outskill frontend-slides). One deck for both presenters.

Audience: working professionals, no tech background. Main session 150 minutes, instructor led. The team briefs and runs the breakout separately.

## How each slide is laid out

Every slide has four parts.

1. **ON THE SLIDE**: the exact text the audience sees, in a box. Give feedback on this part.
   - A line in CAPITALS at the top is the small label above the headline.
   - The next line is the headline. Words inside `==double equals==` get the lime highlight.
   - Lines starting with "Source:" are the small print at the bottom.
   - Lines in [square brackets] describe a visual, not text.
2. **SPEAKER NOTES**: what you say while the slide is up. Taken from Dileep's script. Vishwas variants are marked.
3. **FACILITATOR NOTES**: what you do. Clicks, demos, checks. Never on screen.
4. **CONTEXT**: script section, slide type and layout. For the HTML build.
